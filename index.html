About

Why the movement needs a shared measure

LC-MPBI is a decentralised yet standardised programme. Every national Lean Construction institute can run its own market-penetration study using a common framework based on the rigorous German model — generating comparable data on visibility, adoption, maturity, and impact while keeping full national ownership.

Germany 2025 is the first published national picture and the reference design other countries adapt.

How to participate

One champion. One national picture. Six steps.

Open the registration form

Step 1

Register as National Champion

Nominate yourself or a team. Steering reviews suitability, capacity, and commitment.

Step 2

Download the complete toolkit

German-model instrument: maturity stages, 4L4D frame, survey design used in 2025.

Step 3

Run the survey in your country

Start when ready. Timing is voluntary — ownership stays national.

Step 4

Submit results

Use the shared template so headline figures sit on the same dashboard.

Step 5

See your country on the live dashboard

Visibility, adoption, maturity, and impact — beside every other country.

Step 6

Publish at a Lean Construction conference

Take the national picture to IGLC or a national congress.

A National Champion can be an individual or team from a national LCI, university, research institute, government body, or committed practitioners. Multiple champions per country are welcome.
