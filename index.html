About

Why the movement needs a shared measure

LC-MPBI is a decentralised yet standardised programme. Every national Lean Construction institute can run its own market-penetration study using a common framework based on the rigorous German model — generating comparable data on visibility, adoption, maturity, and impact while keeping full national ownership.

Germany 2025 is the first published national picture and the reference design other countries adapt.

Resources

A shared instrument that countries can adapt

Country Lead Toolkit

The practical package for running a national study with the German model. Read the summary below, then download the working documents when you are ready to start.

What's inside

Method Guide — how to run Phase 1 (industry scan), Phase 2 (interviews), and Phase 3 (nationwide survey), with room for local adaptation.

Survey Template — core questionnaire. Wording may be adapted; the locked metrics stay the same so results remain comparable.

Interview Guide — themes and example questions for practitioner interviews.

Industry Scan Checklist — sources and steps to identify Lean companies in your country.

Results Template — format for submitting headline figures to the LC-MPBI dashboard.

One-pager — maturity stages and the 4L4D frame for quick reference.

Core metrics (locked across countries)

These stay the same in every national study so numbers can sit on one dashboard:

  • Five maturity stages — Informed → Piloting → Selective standard → Majority of projects → Culture & strategy
  • Share of projects using Lean — e.g. <10%, 10–25%, 25–50%, >50%, all
  • Project phases — planning, execution, or both
  • Company profile basics — size, role in the supply chain, years applying Lean
  • 4L4D frame — the synthesis model from the German 2025 study (levels and dimensions of application)

National questions may be added. Core metrics are not removed or redefined.

How to run it
  1. Register as Country Lead and receive access to the full pack.
  2. Download the Method Guide and Survey Template.
  3. Run Phase 1 — industry scan — to build the national list of Lean companies.
  4. Optional: Phase 2 interviews to refine local wording and context.
  5. Launch Phase 3 survey; keep core metrics intact.
  6. Submit headline figures with the Results Template; full publications go under Resources.
